TIME — functional spec SPEC · Cadence

STATUS: WORKING DRAFT (2026-06-16). Functional spec for the TIME family (worked axis: Cadence). Product altitude (the WHAT, not the HOW). Produced by the A→B bootstrap path as bounded Work-Atoms (write → critique → fix), rendered deterministically. Spine: D62 P1 (directional/bounded/structured) + P2 (placement).

A Cadence is read at SHAPE (when must this run, by when is it due), watched at RUN as the clock advances, captured at RECORD as on-time-or-late, flagged at EVIDENCE when lateness crosses a grace margin, and aggregated at GROW into a health signal that GOVERN consults at the next gate — surfacing the schedule shape and the over-time dynamics without ever silently catching up the work itself.

1. Abstract

*TIME is the family of axes that answer WHEN — when work must run, by when it is due, and how the whole system behaves as those clocks tick across many runs.* Every axis here passes one sorting question: is it about when, or about behavior over time? That is its single shared signature, and it is what separates TIME from the five siblings that ask what-kind, what-accumulated, what-is-gated, what-link, or what-stock. A note on naming: the canon time page calls TIME 'one axis with two faces' — that phrasing names the shared schedule/dynamics signature every member carries, not a count of one; here TIME is read as the family whose members all wear that two-face signature, consistent with the master family-table that lists TIME alongside its five siblings. TIME reads two things — the work graph with its durations, and the loop's own stocks and delays — and it fires in two moments: at the front as a plan (schedule), and across the loop as observed motion (dynamics). The operator gets a system that knows the difference between fast and on-time, that names the one path whose length actually sets the deadline, and that warns when a steady-looking machine is quietly oscillating. This family makes time a thing the work is read against, not a thing that merely happens to it.

2. What it does

TIME gives the operator a clock the work answers to. When a job has a due date, the system already knows which deadline is real and which is slack — it surfaces the single critical path so that speeding up an off-path step is correctly understood as changing nothing, and effort goes where the deadline actually lives. When a job recurs, the operator sets its cadence in plain terms and the system holds the rhythm, treating a missed beat as a visible diagnostic rather than an invisible gap. And when many runs stack up over time, TIME watches the loop's own behavior — backlogs that burst-finish then fall idle then burst again — and raises an oscillation warning even when every individual atom finished green. The operator stops discovering lateness after the fact and starts seeing the schedule shape and the rhythm health while there is still room to decide what to do about them.

3. Why this is its own family

The problem TIME solves is that when-ness and over-time behavior hide inside everything yet belong to nothing else: a deadline is not the work, a rhythm is not an authority, an oscillation is not a resource shortage. It earns a closed slot because its sorting question — is this about when, or about dynamics over time — catches a class of concern no sibling's question catches, and because temporal reasoning has a distinct character: it is plan-forward and graph-shaped, not a fresh per-input read and not an accumulated tally. Six families is a principled count — the closed skeleton the sorting test fills — and TIME is the load-bearing sixth: drop it and scheduling collapses into hand-waving and dynamics into surprise. It is its own family because the question 'when, and how over time' is orthogonal to all five others and cannot be folded into any of them without losing the answer. That it reads as 'one axis with two faces' on the canon time page is the same claim from the inside — a single shared signature — and 'family' is that signature seen from the master table; the two readings name the same thing, not a disagreement about how many axes live here.

4. Place in the loop

On the shared loop — INTENT, SHAPE, RUN, RECORD, EVIDENCE, GROW, with GOVERN wrapping every transition — TIME lights up almost the whole arc, with the highlight on its two firing moments. It reads the work graph and the loop's stocks-and-delays, then acts as a plan at SHAPE (the schedule face: when must this run, by when is it due) and as observed motion from RUN through GROW (the dynamics face: how is the rhythm actually behaving). RECORD captures whether each beat landed on time; EVIDENCE flags the misses past grace; GROW aggregates beats into a health signal. GOVERN, wrapping all of it, consults that health at the next gate — a healthy rhythm passes on audit-only, a faltering one can require a human decision. The map never changes; only this family's highlight moves to show TIME plans at the front and watches across the whole loop.

5. The axes

TIME holds an open set of when-and-dynamics axes, each carrying its own coordinates: what it reads, when it fires, and the state ladder it moves through. The fixed skeleton is small and canon-named: two faces — schedule (over the work graph) and dynamics (over the loop) — with Cadence as the worked member that rides the schedule/dynamics signature. Everything below those is open flesh: this is the family explicitly minting members, and the rows marked *(minted — illustrative)* are freshly assimilated this pass under the open-axis rule, not a settled registry. Each axis is marked as a categorical thing-to-sort-on or a numeric thing-to-aggregate, the way a reporting dimension differs from a measure.

6. One axis, walked

Take Cadence, the most load-bearing TIME axis, and watch one value move across the loop. Takeaway first: a cadence does not catch itself up — it surfaces lateness as a number and hands the decision to authority. A recurring payroll job is given a fortnightly rhythm at SHAPE, expressed as a plain recurrence the operator can read; the operator also sets this rhythm's degraded threshold to k = 3 consecutive misses (k is policy-defined per rhythm, not a fixed family constant — see the open-k note below). The loop holds it; for several cycles the beat lands on time and RECORD marks each on-time. Then one cycle the beat is late — RECORD captures the lateness, EVIDENCE checks it against the grace margin and finds it past, so it is flagged rather than swallowed: that is miss 1 of the operator-set 3. A second and third miss follow (misses 2 and 3); GROW aggregates the run of misses into a health state that flips from healthy to degraded exactly when the count reaches this rhythm's k of 3 — the third consecutive miss. Now the highlight reaches GOVERN: at the next gate, authority reads the degraded health and, because this rhythm is load-bearing, requires a human decision before the next run proceeds. At no point did the system silently fire the missed beats — it counted, flagged, and escalated. The static Cadence row from the catalog has become a running one: a rhythm set, a lateness measured, a health degraded at the operator's chosen k, a gate raised. Open-k note: the threshold is operator-set and inherits canon's still-open pattern-emergence-k status (PROPOSED, not ratified); k = 3 here is this rhythm's policy choice, illustrative of the dial, not a fixed value the family asserts.

7. Boundaries — what this family is NOT

TIME owns when and over-time behavior, and nothing else; each near-miss below is converted, flipped to the sibling whose sorting question truly claims it, and flagged with the reason. The mandatory handoffs: scheduling contention is RESOURCE seen over time — when two jobs collide for one finite pool at the same moment, the collision is a stock problem, owned by RESOURCE, even though it shows up on a calendar; the coupling is real and named on both sides (a TIME deadline can be missed because a RESOURCE pool was dry). A deadline that triggers an ask-or-act decision is not TIME's to gate — TIME measures the lateness, GOVERN renders auto/ask/deny, so cadence-health feeds the gate but does not own it. A rhythm hardening into team doctrine ('we always run this weekly') has left the when-question and entered GROW, which owns accumulation-into-practice. TIME states the schedule shape and the dynamics warning; it does not guarantee on-time delivery or stability — a fully-verified atom set can still miss its deadline or oscillate, and that honesty is a boundary, not a gap.

8. Why this grouping

These axes group as one family because they share a single sorting answer — when, or dynamics over time — and splitting them would scatter that answer across slots that ask different questions. The rejected alternative was to fold scheduling into RESOURCE (since contention looks like a stock fight) and dynamics into GROW (since both watch many runs); both were rejected because the unifying question is temporal, not stock-based or accumulation-based — contention is a stock problem only incidentally clocked, and dynamics is about the shape of motion, not the hardening of practice. Merging TIME away would lose the one place that distinguishes fast from on-time and steady-looking from oscillating. The grouping rule is the same closed-skeleton, open-flesh principle the whole system uses, the faceted-scheme discipline of Ranganathan's PMEST applied here: the family is fixed (the two canon faces plus the worked Cadence), the when-axes minted within it are open (Lateness, Cadence-health, and any future rhythm or stability signal).

9. Path to productization

A new TIME axis grows along the same reusable path every axis follows — name and define it, show its need-advantage-how-example, thread it onto the owning time page, cross-link it into the loop and its neighbors, and ratify it — specialized here by where it threads and what ratifies it. A minted when-axis (the section-5 illustrative rows are this path mid-stride) threads onto the family's owning time material and is cross-linked into its two firing moments (plan at the front, dynamics across the loop) and to its named neighbors in RESOURCE and GOVERN; it is ratified as a directional decision that fixes which face it belongs to and states its honest ceiling (diagnostics now, early-warning later). The open-set claim is concrete: a new rhythm, a new lateness measure, or a new stability signal has a known route in, and the family closes at its two faces only until real usage forces the core author to reshape the skeleton. This is the loop's own GROW stage applied to TIME itself — the family that was defined, placed, cataloged, bounded, and justified now shows how it grows. Detail of the generic method lives in the axis-productization material; this stays at the what-and-why.
