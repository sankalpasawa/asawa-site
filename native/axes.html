Axes + families OVERVIEW · sub-page

WORKING DRAFT (2026-06-15). An axis is a dimension you read a piece of work against. Axes are an open set — minted on demand from one primitive (value ↔ axis). What is fixed is the primitive + the families (the slots an axis falls into).

The six families, one home each. This page is the theory; each family's functional spec and, where one exists, its dimension page sit under it. What each family must do first is on what comes first.

FamilyWhat it coversFunctional specDimension pageCapabilities ruled now
SHAPEwhat the work is for, and how we know it is rightspec—Goal, Check
GROWwhat accumulates across runsspec—Run log, Versions, Lineage
GOVERNwho may do what; what needs a yesspec—Auto inside the record, Ask and stamp, Deny, Undo, with severity
EDGEhow pieces connect through documentsspecdimensionNamed reads and writes
RESOURCEwhat a run may spend, per kindspecdimensionCap per run, Chain brake
TIMEwhen work runsspecdimensionRun on request, Schedule, Wake on a new version
As used on the department screen, 2026-09-21. The six families are the closed slots an axis falls into; axes stay an open set. SHAPE reads the input, fresh every run (type, certainty, lens), and sets the output the work must move (impact, ruled 2026-09-25). GROW accumulates on the unit across runs (substance, scale, cross-unit, codify). GOVERN gates a transition (auto, ask, deny). EDGE reads the link between two atoms (hand-off, shared resource, must-cohere). RESOURCE is a finite stock the work spends, spent at run and reconciled at record. TIME is when and over time (schedule and dynamics). No family outranks another. Work is ordered by the moment each family fires in the loop, by the pick test on every axis (keep, merge, rename, reject, escalate; only escalate touches the families), and by the five-step path P1 to P5 that makes a kept axis part of the product. The three moves of the one primitive mint the axes the families sort. Realization moves down, cutting a value into buildable parts, and stops at atomicity. Reflection moves up, lifting a record line to the rule it is an instance of, and stops at the rung that describes itself (M6, reflexive). Reframe moves across, the same value on another axis. Reflexive is the stop of the up move, not a third move. On the screen (a reading, not a ruling): SHAPE is an engine's steps; GROW is Adaptation and the meters; GOVERN is Identity's rules and the asks in Now; EDGE is Coordination's locks and hand-offs; RESOURCE is Priority's budget and the Spend meter; TIME is Priority's queue, each engine's schedule and Waits. Screen: the department screen · the life cycle.

Three principles from nature, in plain words

A reading, not a ruling (2026-09-23). It explains why the families and moves above are shaped the way they are, using how living things and long-lived systems build large structure from very little. Nothing here changes a family or a move.

PrincipleIn plain wordsIn natureA real systemIn a lending companyIn Native
1 Few, independent axesdescribe everything with a handful of dials that do not depend on each othera body is laid out on three axes: head to tail, back to belly, left to rightevery colour on a screen is three dials: red, green, blueevery loan is four dials: amount, tenure, rate, risk band. A new product is a new setting of the dials, not a new designbuilt: six families, and "No family outranks another"
2 One rule that recursesone rule, applied again at every level, grows the whole structurea fern: each leaf is a small copy of the whole frondfolders on a Mac: a folder holds files and folders, and that one rule makes the whole diskholding, company, department, team: at every level the same four things, a goal, an owner, rules, a budgetshape built, rule proposed: every department carries the same five functions, but no single rule yet makes a department the same way at every level
3 Each part reads its own positionnobody tells each part what to be; it looks at where it sits and works it outa cell in an embryo reads the chemical level around it and becomes skin or boneGPS: every phone works out its own position; no server tells each phone where it isa loan officer's approval limit comes from the seat, not the person. Move them and the limit moves with the seatbuilt: a department picks its function templates from what it does, such as Money movement if it moves money

Principle 2, pictured on the holding itself. The same four things repeat at every level, and nothing else is needed to grow the tree:

Native has principles 1 and 3. Principle 2 is the missing piece: one rule that makes a department identically at every level. That rule is what the root department's start engine would be, and it is proposed, not built.

The sorting test

ask of any axis:
  reads the INPUT, fresh every run?        -> SHAPE
  accumulates on the UNIT, across runs?    -> GROW
  gates a transition (auto/ask/deny)?      -> GOVERN
  reads the LINK between two atoms?         -> EDGE
  a finite STOCK the work spends?           -> RESOURCE
  about WHEN / over time?                   -> TIME
