Archived 2026-09-26. Merged on the founder's direction into the system, grown in this order. Replaced by The product, in the order it is built, whose screens follow the ruled order instead of adding one family per step. Kept below as it was.

What a person sees, screen by screen, as the product gains the six families one at a time. The same Collections department on every screen: a rail on the left, the list of work in the middle, the selected piece of work on the right. Whatever a step adds is outlined in yellow.

Founder's direction, 2026-09-26: show how the six families are added one by one, how the design grows step by step, and what product that design becomes, as visuals rather than theory, to rework over and over. The design behind these screens is from one step to a company. Illustrative data; nothing here is built as drawn.

2 · How we build it, one screen at a time

Each screen is one slice to build, in this order, and each is finished only when its last column holds on a real run.

#AddsThe person can nowWe buildDone when
1the seedrun one piece of work and see what it read and wroteartifact versions with inputs and check; one runner; the run logone real run writes a versioned artifact naming its inputs and check
2the departmentsee all the work and what reads whatwiring of reads and writes; wakes with a root and a hop countone new loan book runs the chain, no piece twice
3SHAPEsee what matters most, firstgoal, check and impact on each recipe; the rankingthe order matches the owner's own in 8 of 10
4GOVERNapprove before anything leavesasks with stamps; ask again when an input is uncheckedno call leaves without a stamp or a stamped rule
5EDGEsee what waits on what; reject a bad inputpublish rules at junctions; reject; asks for cross-department readsa junction publishes only when both inputs are in
6RESOURCEsee and cap what work spendscaps per run; the department ceilingwork waits when its cap is full
7TIMEsee when things wake and what is overdueschedules, wake on a new version, windowsan overdue reply escalates with no one watching
8GROWwatch parts grow; stamp or refuse each steprecipes as artifacts; proposals from signals; stepping downa stamped proposal writes the next recipe and the next run uses it
9the companysee grown and young departments togetherdepartments as work you can open; shared artifactsa young department reads a grown one's artifact on its first day
One department, one screen, six families added one at a time. Each family arrives as a column in the list, a section in the panel, and at most one thing in the rail or the top bar.
