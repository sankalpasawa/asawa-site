The engineering band answers HOW Native is built. It splits three ways. Group 1 — Per-part: 13 pages, one per PLATFORM block, "how THIS part works" (mechanisms + behavior contracts). Group 2 — Cross-cutting HLD views: 6 whole-system views that span ALL parts and answer a question no single part page answers (data model, control flow, interfaces, governance, deployment, non-functional).
