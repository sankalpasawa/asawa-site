What this layer is — and what it is not. Three documents touch B4’s engineering, each at a different scope: (1) the matrix states B4’s pillar×part traceability — PRIMARY in External World, SUPPORTING across UI/Orchestration/SoP/Compute — but no flow; (2) the per-part pages (Connector, External World) carry each part’s local mechanism rows — how transport dispatches, what the counterparties are — but each is blind to the other half; (3) this page owns the whole-pillar design: the invariants that must hold across both parts and the properties that are non-local (true only of the end-to-end path, not of either part alone). Per the one-home principle (BUILD-PROTOCOL p7), every mechanism row below is CITED to its owning part page or canon file — this page restates none of them; it states only the cross-part relations between them.

Cross-part invariants — what must hold ACROSS the parts (the whole-pillar contract)

These are the contracts that no single part page owns, because each is blind to the other half. The matrix gives traceability but no invariant; the per-part pages give local mechanism but not the relation between them. This is the layer’s reason to exist (deepseek P2 hole).

#Invariant (cross-part)Why it is non-local (spans ≥2 parts)StatusGrounding (cited)
I1Typed-envelope contract held end-to-end. A B4 message is a typed envelope on BOTH lanes: outbound, the draft becomes a typed envelope at the Connector before it reaches the External World counterparty; inbound, the counterparty payload is wrapped into a typed envelope before it routes to B3. The same typed-envelope discipline must hold across the outbound seam (SoP→Connector→World) and the inbound seam (World→Connector→B3).The Connector page states the envelope on each lane locally; the External World page states the counterparties. Neither states that the SAME envelope shape must survive the round-trip across both part seams — that binding is the pillar-level fact.HELD (master-spec)cited: conn mech-02 (§5.2 flow) · conn mech-04 (B17 typed Connector) · ext-world neighbor-edge contracts · master §5.2
I2Retry / idempotency boundary. The Connector retries transient failures; the runtime defines DEGRADED reconciliation as "idempotent retry OR manual log-write". But the idempotency KEY that would make a retry safe across the part boundary is not defined: B16 A2A is canon-silent on ack_id replay / idempotency dedup, and no idempotency key is named for the Slack/email/web lanes. A retry after a half-fail (send succeeded, audit missed) can therefore double-deliver into the External World.The retry lives in the Connector; the duplicate lands in the External World; the DEGRADED state lives in SoP. The dir/D29 identity-concealment invariant — no Asawa/Sutra brand strings in author-visible metadata, skill contents, or first-run UX — governs what a counterparty can see. So the outbound voice (B4 audience calibration, owned in SoP/B4) and the transport headers/metadata (owned in Connector) must BOTH be clean of platform-brand leakage before a message crosses into the External World.The brand could leak at the voice layer (SoP/B4) OR the transport layer (Connector) OR the envelope metadata that lands in the External World. The A2A stub as a cross-part concern (transport + counterparty, spanning both)

Peer-Native A2A is the clearest case where the pillar — not either part — is the right home: the counterparty (a peer Sutra instance) lives in the External World page, while the transport (the A2A envelope on the wire) lives in the Connector page, yet in v1 there is no live transport at all — only a log-only stub that records intent. The B16 A2AEnvelope {src_native_id, dst_native_id, msg_type, payload, ack_id, agent_identity_chain} (all 6 fields required per Q35) is the typed-envelope invariant I1 applied to the peer-Sutra case; the missing ack_id dedup is invariant I2 applied to it. Full transport is gated v2+ behind four §1.4.5 activation gates (B2.c peer Sutras live · A2A protocol authored as ADR · cross-Native identity/auth model · audit extends to cross-Native DecisionProvenance). B16 vs B17 per canon: B16 = internal Native↔Native; external counterparties = Connectors (B17). Canon linked, never copied (D54): sutra/os/native/blocks/B16-native-native-a2a.md.

Open gaps — founder queue (cross-part holes this layer surfaces)

    I2 — retry/idempotency boundary undefined (the headline cross-part hole). The Connector retries transient fails and DEGRADED reconciliation allows "idempotent retry", but no idempotency key is defined for the Slack/email/web lanes and B16 is canon-silent on ack_id replay/idempotency dedup — so a retry across the part seam can double-deliver. Owning fix-site spans Connector (retry) + SoR (audit) + SoP (DEGRADED). (cited: conn mech-09; ext-world B16 gap; B16-native-native-a2a.md)
    I3 — white-label boundary not stated as a B4 transport constraint. dir/D29 ships an identity-concealment invariant + leak-audit.sh for the PLUGIN tree, but Native states no rule that B4’s outbound envelopes/headers/voice must be brand-clean before they cross into the External World. Should the D29 invariant extend to runtime connector metadata, or is it publish-time only? (cited: SHIPPED-SOLUTIONS.md#dir/D29; FOUNDER-DIRECTIONS.md D29)
    I4 — inbound Tenancy mechanism unstated. §5.2/§5.3 name only Identity-verify on inbound; whether the Tenancy half of the Authority+Tenancy wrap applies to inbound external messages, and via what mechanism, is an open question carried from the External World page. (cited: ext-world open gaps)
    Latency — no per-send outbound SLA. The only stated B4 timing budget is the ≤60s notify-on-change edge; canon states no per-message outbound send latency target nor a retry-backoff timing budget. The pillar therefore has a partial latency budget only. (cited: conn mech-11; no master anchor for outbound send SLA)
    B4 Ring 1 Surface rows "Awaiting founder fill" (Problem space / Need-outcome / Solution direction — T-G1-11/12/13); Phase 2 design connector-comms-design.md pending (T-G3-04). Could founder voice shift the PRIMARY assignment (B4 home block = External World) once filled? (cited: master #pillar-b4; matrix open gaps)
    Deferred counterparty activation: full peer-Native A2A transport gated v2+ behind 4 §1.4.5 gates; phone connector DEFERRED (T-G2-24..27, v2+); browser co-work DEFERRED (T-G2-28..31 / Charter TODO-005, v1.x). These are pillar-spanning because each adds BOTH a counterparty (External World) AND a lane (Connector). (cited: master §1.4.5)
