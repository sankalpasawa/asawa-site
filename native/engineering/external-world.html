Second-order internal flow — parallel counterparties, two one-way Connector lanessecond-order-draftThe parts of the External World do not connect to each other — they are parallel counterparties, each reached over its own Connector channel. Traffic flows in two one-way lanes through the Connector transport (§5.2): on the outbound lane, System of Process output (B3's decision, B4's draft) passes the Authority gate, becomes a typed envelope with retry logic, and lands on a service endpoint (Slack, email, web, 3rd-party tool) or, through Slack/email, on a human stakeholder. On the inbound lane, a human's reply (e.g. the board chair's Slack response) is identity-verified, wrapped into a typed envelope, and routed back to B3 as the next action — so people and services only 'meet' inside System of Process, never inside the External World block. Peer Sutras have only a log-only A2A stub edge in v1 (no real transport), and browser/phone counterparts have no live edge at all. At the §1.0 altitude the whole block collapses to a single bidirectional 'context + effects' edge with System of Process, with Authority + Tenancy wrapping every crossing. The draft's System of Process neighbor stub states 'Connector lanes live here (§5.2)' — matching the manifest acceptance line 'Connector lanes live in SoP; External World owns counterparties' (codex MODIFY-4), with platform/connector.html cited for transport.holding/website/native/_block-external-world.html (Internal flow + SoP neighbor stub)

Neighbor contracts — SoP edges + Authority/Tenancy wrap on every crossingsecond-order-draftThe contracts table declares four neighbor edges. System of Process (bidirectional): 'context + effects' per the §1.0 edge label — SoP gathers context FROM the External World and pushes effects INTO it; this is one of the two external edges of the runtime (the other being System of Record, which gets 'context + output'). System of Process (in): outbound sends via the Connector outbound lane (B3 → B4) — audience-calibrated draft converted to a typed envelope, Authority-gated at every send, retry logic on transient fail, DecisionProvenance row per send; permanent failures surface to operator, no silent loss. System of Process (out): inbound receives via the Connector inbound lane (B4 → B3) — identity-verified, payload converted to a typed envelope, routed to B3 as next action; inbound from unverified identity logs + drops and never enters B3; inbound responses re-enter the loop via B3 and prior comms become B2 future context. Authority + Tenancy (gates): every outbound call passes the Authority gate with default-deny on missing scope; per-tool Charter scoping enforced (e.g. 'Slack:#paisa-team only', not 'Slack:*'); B4 adds per-stakeholder + tool + $-threshold gating on who Native may talk to.holding/website/native/_block-external-world.html (Contracts — neighbor edges table)
