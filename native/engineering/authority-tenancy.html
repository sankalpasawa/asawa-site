By domain: Native acts in one company's domain but not another (per Charter). By tool: Native can call Slack but not bank-API without auth. By stakeholder: Native emails team but escalates board/regulator. By dollar threshold: ≤$X auto, >$X ask. By reversibility: reversible auto, irreversible ask.
