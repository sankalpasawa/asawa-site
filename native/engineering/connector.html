§5 layer contract — purpose, owns, depends-on, does-not-ownmaster-spec§5 Connector is the transport layer: purpose is to move messages + tool calls between Native (§4) and the world outside (humans, services, peer Sutras). It owns Slack · email · web · browser · phone · 3rd-party tool integrations · inbound/outbound message handling · retry semantics.
