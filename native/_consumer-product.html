Operator-visible states — the states strip

StateWhat the operator seesDoc home
error Operator-visible failure surfaces: misclassified-intent confirm-back with yes/no/clarify (G.1, one focused question max); failed action stated plainly in operator words with 3 recovery options, no stack traces or codes (G.2 retry-now/retry-later/cancel, wait-and-retry/make-copy/cancel, retry-misses/show-which/leave-as-is); operator overrides “stop” / “undo” / “different approach” (G.3); wrong-answer retry loop where two consecutive misses escalate to operator walkthrough (G.4); every correction visibly acknowledged and carried forward, never silent (G.5). section-2-f-7-errors-recovery
empty The “I don't know what to ask for” surface. On-demand: structured capability map organized by job (5 JTBDs), by domain, and by stage — live today / available-unused / unlocks-on-grant (H.1). Before the operator opens the surface: Daily Pulse (H.3). Proactive surfacing on adjacent-ask, repeated-manual (3x), and dormant-fit triggers — soft offers that never block and decay after two declines (H.2). Re-engagement when dormant: “no new asks in 5 days” surfaces a dormant capability framed against current pain (I.6). section-2-f-8-capability-pulse
onboarding First session (~10 min): light charter setup + quick persona read (P1–P4) + a first REAL job, not a tutorial — by minute 10 one piece of actual work is done and the loop is felt once (I.1). Then staged: Day 1 (first job + quick follow-ups, directives queued, I.2), Week 1 (charter ratifies, first 2–3 standing directives fire, first Daily Pulse lands, I.3), Month 1 (mature ask-act-report-learn loop, OHS 3–6 band, I.4), Day 90+ (habit-formed, I.5), with drop-off triggers + recovery moves (I.6: pulse unopened 3 days → shrink to 3 lines; routine turned off → confirm, log why, offer leaner version). section-2-f-9-lifecycle
notification Outbound landings the operator did not just ask for: scheduled deliveries at a stated moment (08:00 digest, “ping me Friday”, M6 scheduled reminder) and async completion reports with receipt confirmed up front (D.2); Slack ping M2 when a standing routine completed or operator asked for Slack delivery; escalation override promotes the channel one step (chat → Slack DM → SMS-tier) and notes why, only when time-sensitive AND default channel is async (D.3). Output discipline: default to least-noise (D intro). Cadence/quiet-hours bounds come from the charter (E.2). section-2-f-4-outputs
