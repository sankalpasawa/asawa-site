The Root Department PROPOSAL · 3 PICTURES

The machine ships with one department already in it. It is never shown. It holds the five functions like any other, and it holds the engines that make, change and end every department the person will ever have. Founder's direction, 2026-09-23. Nothing is built; this page is the design.

1 · What ships, and what is seen

Dashed grey = present but never drawn in the tree. Green = the person's own departments. Two hidden children keep two different jobs apart: System is the app looking after itself, Mac is the machine it runs on.

PartWhat it holdsWhy it is hidden
rootthe five functions, and the engines that create, change and retire departmentsnobody manages the machine as a department; they manage their work
Systemthe app's own upkeep: updates, backups, health, the placement lintit is the product maintaining itself, not the person's work
Macwhat runs on this machine: disk, power, network, the calendar jobs that fire enginessame reason; it becomes visible the day a person asks why something did not run

2 · The one new rule this forces

The model already says: the Identity above judges the thing changing. Root has nothing above it, so either the rule breaks or it terminates somewhere. It terminates in a person.

Authority ends at the person. Root's own Identity is judged by whoever is at the machine; every other Identity is judged by the department above it. That is the whole chain: person → root → their departments → those departments' children.

Two consequences worth stating before anything is built. Root can never be retired, which the record already enforces. And a person cannot be removed from root without removing themselves from the machine, so root's owner line is the one place the product must never let an agent write.

3 · How every department is born, once root exists

One path, with an owner and a run behind it. The person still stamps; what changes is that the making is a department's work, recorded, instead of an act of the app.

TodayThrough root
two birth paths, unreconciled: an ask from the tree, and a department screen's own birth askone path: root's engine files the ask, whoever asked for it
the app mints a department per project folder at start-up, unstamped, unrecordedthat import is one of root's engines; its runs are rows, and it may be turned off
a birth leaves a stamped ask and nothing about what caused ita birth leaves a run, the line that caused it, and the stamp
retiring a department is reachable only from a sessionroot's retire engine files the ask; root is the default successor for anything left over

4 · Which of the eight states root supplies

Root owns the two ends of the life — the birth and the ending — plus the first engine that makes a new department able to act at all. The middle stays where it belongs, with the department itself.

That is the life-cycle answer: the two states nothing owns today (state 3, able to act, and state 8, ended) both become root's work. The eight-state walk would then reach all eight, because an engine could be created by an engine rather than only by a person at the app.

5 · How it grows

MomentWhat happensWho acts
First run of the approot, System and Mac exist; the tree looks empty because nothing of the person's is there yetthe installer
The first line the person writesroot's engine turns it into a department with Identity, a folder and a first engineroot, the person stamps
Every later departmentthe same engine, whether asked for from the tree, from a chat, or by another department's Adaptationroot
A department outgrows itselfit proposes a child; root's engine does the minting, the parent's owner stampsthe department, then root
A department endsroot's retire engine names a successor and files the ask; nothing is deletedroot, the person stamps
The machine itself needs workSystem and Mac run their own engines: updates, backups, health, the jobs that fire everything elseroot's children

6 · What it fixes, and what it does not

#Open problemRoot's answer
1The app mints departments at start-up with nobody stampingfixed: it becomes root's import engine, with runs and an off switch
2Ending a department is unreachable from the appfixed: root's retire engine files the ask; root is the fallback successor
3Two unreconciled birth pathsfixed: one engine, whatever asked for it
4A new department cannot act until a person writes its first enginefixed: root's engine files the first engine ask with the birth
5Nothing detects a department whose functions never ticknot fixed by root itself, but root's Audit is the obvious home for the check
6Owner and budget are text that nothing enforcesnot fixed: an enforced owner is a record change, wherever the department sits
Root is not a new idea in the model. It is the model applied to the machine itself — the same five functions, the same asks, the same runs, one level above everything the person sees.

7 · The calls this needs

#CallThe two ways
1One hidden node or threeroot alone, with system work as its own engines; or root with System and Mac as hidden children
2Whether root is ever visiblenever; or behind a show-system switch for the day something does not run
3Existing installsadopt today's top-level departments under root at first launch; or leave them and let root hold only what comes next
4Who may write root's rulesthe person only, with agents refused at the record; or an agent may propose and the person stamps
5What Mac's engines may doread-only reporting on disk, power and jobs; or acting on them, which makes the app an administrator of the machine
