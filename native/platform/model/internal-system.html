Purpose

The Internal System is the five glue engines — coordination, priority, audit, adaptation, identity — present pre-provisioned inside every system. The charter states what must be true; the internal system is the machinery that KEEPS it true. Ruled in the A1 family (founder sittings 2026-08-05/06): glue engines are engines too — the same four-store engine shape as any Engine (canonical owner: Workflow + Engine s2) pointed at other engines instead of at domain work. They hold the composition state (axiom X3: nothing floats outside an engine — the coordination engine owns who-may-call-whom, the priority engine owns budgets). The internal system exists so a system's upkeep is made of the same stuff as its work (engines all the way down), and so growth stays bounded: the five slots are hardcoded from birth, empty is fine.

Where the five come from. Stafford Beer's Viable System Model: S5 identity, S4 intelligence, S3 control, S3* audit, S2 coordination. The research behind this page, unit by unit, with what Beer says we should add and where he argues against us: VSM: the five units.

How it works

The record→evidence boundary the audit + adaptation glue engines read.

The two growth ladders — scale (slots filling) and substance (C-states hardening) — are orthogonal. Fuller v2 treatments: Maturation and Crystallization (v2 pages; canonical home pending a founder call — see the fix report's queue).

Evidence drives four growths — each write-back lands in a different glue engine's territory.

The maturation loop — how the record's named pains fill the internal-system slots (human-gated).

Glue is not a new object kind. Each slot, when filled, is a grown Engine — born at its workflow's first run (X2), state as explicit inputs from the record, never hidden. What makes it glue is its target: it runs above the work engines and is owned by none of them. Three ruled properties complete the mechanics:

  • Regress stop. There is no internal system of the internal system. The glue engines are crystallized invariants — their verifies ARE their governance; code invariants ARE the governance. The recursion ends here by construction.
  • Pre-provisioning (Ruling 1). Every system gets its charter and five internal-system slots hardcoded from birth, even empty — growth stays bounded, nothing evolves into undefined shapes. The 163 auto-minted charter stubs in the build are validated as intentional structure, not debt.
  • Crystallization speed. Glue engines are mostly CODE — they crystallize fastest of anything in the model. The maturation ladder (S1→S5) is exactly "which internal-system slots have filled yet": coordination (S2), priority/resource (S3), audit (S3*), adaptation (S4), identity (S5).

Ownership and failure modes

The host system owns its internal system; no work engine owns any glue engine (that is the System test). Failure modes attested in the sources: CRITICAL audit finding (exit 1), resource contention between engines (R12), instrument decay — red-never-fixed / green-never-consulted (R10), and self-verifying instruments where writer and checker were the same engine (R8).

Honest ceiling (sheet §8b, carried from ADR-030 D6): T1 closable today; T2 partial (machine runs + records, human picks the question); T3 only inside ADR-010's detector; T4 sensing only. The glue engines for R6/R8/R10 are design-forward — only the daily governance audit (R9) and the docs guards (§8c) run today.

Observability

  • Audit findings append to a findings log; CRITICAL exits 1 (grounding §2).
  • Cadence is scheduled (launchd 09:07 audit, 09:37 triage); pass criterion: mean time-to-visibility for silent failure classes ≤ 1 cadence tick (R9).
  • Every proposal and ruling persists — write-backs leave a proposal + ruling trail (sheet §2, GROW edge).

Purpose

A Shadow System is a structured overlay: a grown System with its own glue engine and its own charter, created when work must cross standing systems in a way their composition does not define. The ruling (founder, Ruling 3, 2026-08-06 sitting) is categorical: do not change the standing systems — create the shadow, let it coordinate them, and let it retire or persist on its own terms. It exists so standing systems can stay "discrete and messy" (Ruling 2) while cross-system work still gets structure. The boundary rule is axiom X4 as amended: standing systems are domain-drawn; shadow systems are use-case-drawn.

How it works

A shadow system is a System in the full sense of the hub's Assembly/System level: it is grown (RUN, never merely FOLLOWed), it has its own goals, constraints, and verify, and per pre-provisioning it gets its charter and internal-system slots hardcoded from birth — empty is fine. What makes it a shadow is its relation to the standing systems it touches: it coordinates ACROSS them and never mutates them.

When to create one — all three conditions:

  • Work must cross two or more standing systems.
  • Their composition does not define the crossing.
  • Therefore the standing systems are NOT changed — the shadow is created instead (Ruling 3).

The crossing mechanism is the delegation edge: granted, revocable, audited. The decision sheet records its shape — derived from Approval plus a typed edge plus two EngineEvents — and consciously defers the specification (A7): in a single-tenant small system the edge never fires; it gets specced when a second tenant exists.

DEFERRED (A7): delegation grant/revoke lifecycle. Shape recorded, spec deferred until a second tenant exists.

Mandates are the canonical example. "Cut cost 15%" does not rewire any department: the mandate becomes a shadow system whose charter carries the rule, whose glue engine coordinates the standing systems, and whose verify is the target. The investor-update path — one artifact touching two departments — is the other named example. Cross-cutting rules generally are the charter property of a shadow system; this frame collapse is what dissolved the earlier charter-as-object argument (recorded in the sheet as explored-and-superseded). A charter holds rules, never work-state.

Retirement: when the shadow system's verify passes, it retires; residue codifies. The codify path is the gated GROW arm — write-backs are always gated, never automatic, and the target is the FOLLOW catalog, where a path that repeated earns its workflow-type noun by repetition. A shadow that persists does so on its own terms.

Things that are connected drift apart silently. A page and its index. A decision sheet and the pages that cite it. A schema and the doc that mirrors it. The edge system makes those connections first-class objects — declared, watched, and honestly flagged when they go stale — so intelligence is spent on the frontier of a change, never on re-reading everything.

On the department screen, ruled 2026-09-20. The edges are drawn as Coordination's Locks and Who may hand to whom, the arrows on Adaptation's map, and Filed work's readers. Screen: the department screen (journeys on it) (locked 2026-09-20; decision sheet section 12) · the life cycle of an engine and a department.

1 · What this is for

The jobKeep documents and the running system bound both ways: a doc change surfaces the implementations it touches; a system change surfaces the doc claims it invalidates. Nothing drifts silently.
Not the jobNot a search index, not a knowledge graph of everything, not automatic rewriting. It never edits content on its own — it flags, and a dial owner clears.
"Correct" meansEvery governed node is reachable from its discovery surfaces (membership) AND no flag is silently lost: a change either fires its edges or the checker fails. Zero silent drift, zero flag spam (idempotent firing).

2 · The whole system in three sentences

1. Everything has a home — a registry row.
2. Everything says what it reads and what lists it — edges.
3. When something changes, its dependents get flagged — and each flag is cleared by code, by an LLM, or by a person, always with evidence.

3 · Born from a real incident

A new page was registered in the site manifest. The manifest checker passed. The sidebar-consistency checker passed. The page still never appeared in the index — the canonical sidebar was hand-maintained, and no edge connected “registered” to “listed.” The founder caught it by eye. The general rule that closes this class of hole:

Consistency checks compare representations. Membership checks prove reachability. A node is not “in the system” until a declared member-of edge makes it reachable from its discovery surfaces — passing consistency alone is instrument theater.

7 · The two mechanisms

Fig 1 · A change walks its edges: the orchestrator watches, the engine records, dials clear, the checker gates.

The Edge Engine (glue engine; owned by no system)

  • Receives firings and opens one flag per dependent edge, recorded on that edge’s system level.
  • Clears only with dial + evidence; refuses empty evidence.
  • Checks: exit-fail while any blocking flag is open; per-system open/blocking counts always derivable from the ledger.
  • Boards: regenerates the human surface from the ledger — never from separate state.

The Orchestrator (the system-level seat)

  • Watches changes since the cursor (commit-based); fires edges for every registered node that changed.
  • Cursor advances only after flags are safely written. An explicit --since never mutates the cursor unless asked.
  • Intended seats: pre-commit chain, CI, cron. A firing is idempotent per edge+commit, so overlapping seats cannot double-flag.

Failure semantics (a conforming build must handle all of these)

FailureRequired behavior
Duplicate firing (re-run, overlapping seats)Idempotency key edge+commit: second firing is a no-op, logged as dedupe.
Partial ledger writeCursor does not advance; next run re-fires; dedupe absorbs the overlap.
Stale/lost cursor (branch switch, fresh clone)Detect non-ancestor base; require explicit --since; never guess.
Registry miss (changed node has no edges)Not an error — lazy grain means ungoverned is legal. Log count only.
Concurrent clears of one flagFirst CLEAR wins — enforced before append: the engine dedupes on flag id + status, so a second CLEAR row is never written (no-op with notice). This keeps first-CLEAR-wins consistent with last-row-wins derivation (§6): the losing clear leaves no row for derivation to pick up.
False-positive edge (fired but dependent unaffected)Legal clear: dial owner records evidence “checked, unaffected.” High false-positive rate on an edge = retire or split the edge.
Evidence unavailable at clear timeFlag stays OPEN. There is no evidence-free clear.

9 · What a person sees

At the moment a thing happens, the card answers five questions without a click: what changed, what it touches, whether it blocks, who owns the fix, and exactly how to clear it:

+-- EDGE FLAG -------------------------------------------------+
| Flag:     f-003      OPEN   [BLOCKING]  age 0m
| System:   governance
| Changed:  holding/bin/sutra-edge
| Reason:   new tool created (commit 5386d17)
| Edge:     e-006 member-of -> SYSTEM-MAP.md  (dial: llm)
| Clear:    sutra-edge clear --flag f-003 --how llm
|           --evidence '<what you did>'
| Hint:     new tool needs a SYSTEM-MAP row
+--------------------------------------------------------------+

The standing surface is the board: one lane per system level; red cards for open blocking flags, amber for advisory, green for recently cleared — each green card showing its evidence. The board is a pure regeneration from the flag ledger. Acceptance for the board: a person who was not present at the change can decide act now / delegate / safely ignore from the card alone.

13 · A conforming implementation must demonstrate

  • The incident replay: register a page, skip the index — the checker fails on membership, not by eye.
  • Idempotency: firing the same change twice yields one flag.
  • Evidence discipline: no flag clears empty; every green card shows its proof.
  • Honest staleness: a fired-but-unpropagated dependent is visibly STALE until cleared.
  • Blocking semantics: check fails on blocking, passes with advisory, and says which system lane.
  • Frontier economics: cost of a change scales with its edges, never with document size.
The whole page in one line: declare the connections once, watch them always, flag honestly, clear only with evidence — and spend intelligence on the frontier of the change, never on the whole document.
