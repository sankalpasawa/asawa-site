Use cases: the journeys of the customers

The meetings face at the organisation level of the structure: the goals people bring to the product, who wants what reality, and when it is done. Each row is one outcome; the journeys with the system (J1, J2, ...) are how the product gets there.

Founder said (2026-10-01): "User journeys with the system, user journeys of the customers." · (2026-09-30): "I'm just trying to make a website live in version 1." · (2026-09-29): "I'm thinking for myself as an operator who is running multiple things, heavily invested, and wants to check on departments and how it's doing."

One use case stands; the second is his own case, sketched; the third box is empty on purpose.
