The first use case: a website for my organisation DESIGN · zoom 4

The founder's cut, 2026-09-29: "I want to create a website for my existing organisation. I want to create a digital footprint. Let's solve for that. Remove all the flab. Create the design experience on top of what we have, as simple as we can."

Say, wait, live: the whole experience in one window. A change loops back through the same box in S3.

The high level (two products, the seven moments, who it is for) stays on Platform journeys. This page is one use case, one window, three states, on top of the Website department that already runs. BUILT is used as it is (a label may differ). MODE shows or hides a built part behind a setting. CAPABILITY is new and small. CONFLICT needs a change to today's logic and waits for the founder.
