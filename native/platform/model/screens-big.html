4 · The five functions

How a function's screens work today (from 20-dept.js and 22-website.js): three tabs. The first tab, named after the function, shows its engines' steps on their rungs (code, checklist, call), each with a Hold switch and a PASSING bar, and "Starts on / unless" and "Hears" chips. Chat is the function's live chat: its own turns with a step row after each one ("Read the request, code · 21:23"). Settings holds the template (Change), the limit (calls, USD a day), the ladder's four numbers (runs before a step climbs, differing runs it may keep, runs a trial lasts, misses that step it down), Starts and Hears. What each function keeps and decides comes from its template (function-templates/<fn>/product-build.json: floor, choices).

Now: the app, Sutra 2.306.10, 2026-09-29

Identity's Chat tab: a step row after every turn; eight of them between two answers.

Priority's Settings tab: template, limit, the ladder, starts, hears.

New: one card per function, in the app's markup

Click a function on the left of the prototype. The card: what it is for; what it keeps (the template's floor, in fewer words); what it decides (the template's choices); what it does with the engines; what it did last. Under it, Settings with only what a person sets: the template, and for Priority the budget. The ladder's four numbers move behind Details.
