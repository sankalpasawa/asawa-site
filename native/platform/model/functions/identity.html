Identity BUILT

hears, judges, files, gates. Identity is the department's voice and its conscience. It hears what a person says (or what Sutra hands over), decides whether the department has enough to work, files the Brief, keeps the rules, and holds the gates that stop anything from leaving the record without the person's word.

One template for all five (founder, 2026-09-30): each function is a system of its own, with its own goal, workflows and journeys, in the box shape; version 1 is one AI agent with .md files inside the covering (S-28). This page is rendered from functions.json; a field the founder has not ruled says placeholder; _functions.py --check refuses a malformed template or a hand edit.
In version 1 (make a site live, nothing more; founder 2026-09-30): take the person's words as the Brief; one question when they name nothing to build from; the first-publish ask stays as the one default; tell what was made and what is live. Nothing else.
