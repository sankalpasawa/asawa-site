Adaptation RULED 2026-09-30 (S-26, S-27, S-28)

the department's intelligence. Identity holds what the department is for and what it may not do. Adaptation decides how the department gets there, and keeps changing how as it learns. It senses, decides, and acts through four hands: it makes workflows, crystallizes steps from AI to code, grows the department out, and learns what worked into the workflow steps. It keeps going until the goal is reached. Every workflow it makes or changes goes through Root's standards.

One template for all five (founder, 2026-09-30): each function is a system of its own, with its own goal, workflows and journeys, in the box shape; version 1 is one AI agent with .md files inside the covering (S-28).
