A Department's Eight States A WALK · RUNNABLE

The life cycle says what the stages are. This page is the walk: what a person does at each state, what they see, and which record proves it. Everything below was produced by running it — seven of the eight states were reached on a throwaway registry, in about a second, with no app open.

The eight states

Green = reached by the run. Dashed red = reachable only through the app. The line returns from state 4 to state 5 because reading is continuous, not a step you finish.

What a person does at each

The run, in its own words

The walk is a script, not a description. It points the registry at a temp folder before it loads the engine, so it never touches anyone's records.

#  state                   proved by   result
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1  Ignited                 record      reached
2  Identity set            record      reached
3  Able to act             app         BLOCKED
4  Working                 record      reached
5  Read against its goal   computed    reached
6  Grown                   record      reached
7  Changed from above      record      reached
8  Ended                   record      reached
------------------------------------------------------------------------
7 of 8 states reached without the app; 1 needs it

State 8 is the one worth reading closely. The engine refuses to end a department while anything under it has nowhere to go, then re-homes everything onto the successor, and the whole move can be reversed:

a retire with no successor is refused: 4 item(s) would be stranded

[8] Ended  --  reached
    status=retired; child re-parented to the successor=True; filed work re-homed=True; recovery manifest=written

    unretire: ok=True; status now active

States 2 and 7 count only after the record is read back. This is what the run prints for them:

[2] Identity set  --  reached
    rules record C-f7c3253e83e65ddf written; goal and scope on it; read back from the record: 2 done lines, 2 tagged rules (go, refuse); folder set=True

[7] Changed from above  --  reached
    new rules version C-2b99ee54062c7c55, superseding C-f7c3253e83e65ddf; the old one is kept; read back: 3 rules on the new version, the added refuse line among them

What the walk exposes

#FindingConsequence
1Seven of eight states are reachable with no app at allthe model is real in the record, not only on screens
2The one state that needs the app is Able to actthe engine is a department's only pair of hands, and it is the one state kept outside the registry
3Ending works, is strict, and is reversiblewhat is missing is one ask, not a mechanism
4Two states pass in the record and read wrong on screena working department looks idle; a rules change shows no version
5The walk needed no chat, no engine run and no networka department's life is a sequence of records; everything else is how a person touches them
Seven of eight states need no app. The eighth is the one that gives a department hands.
