Purpose

The Work-Atom is the smallest unit of work in the ruled model: one goal, ONE verify, 1..n internal steps. It exists so accountability has a floor — an atom is atomic in accountability, not size (RULED, sheet A1-ext). Everything above it composes atoms (a Workflow — the written blueprint — is 1..n atoms; a single atom is a workflow of one, per axiom X1: no naked work), and everything below it is a particle (goal, constraints, context, HOW) that cannot run alone. When the HOW is undecided, the atom defaults to the intelligence floor: hand the whole atom to the LLM (RULED, sheet A1-FINAL). This page is the engineering contract for that unit; the conceptual home is the hub's Work-Atom level.

How it works

What fires an atom's workflow and what feeds its context particle.

The keystone: intent is the whole, each atom-goal a part.

The atom's anatomy — migrated from the v2 page; fourth block relabeled HOW per the ruling (A1-FINAL-3). Block IDs on first use: B7 = pre/post-check block · B9 = persistence block · B11 = PromptBuilder (context construction) · P12 = deterministic-surface-around-stochastic-core principle — see Catalog: Primitives.

An atom is the container of exactly four particles — goal, constraints, context, HOW (A1-FINAL-3). Canonical owner: Particles — this page depends on it for the per-particle definitions and ruled tests.

Atomicity rule (RULED, A1-ext): one goal, ONE verify; 1..n internal steps allowed under that single verify. The moment an internal step needs its own verify, split — two atoms in a workflow.

The verify is split across two parties (sheet R8):

SideWhoMeaning
asserted-donethe executorthe atom's own verify passed on this attempt
accepteda counter-party, via Authoritythe checking engine must not be the writing engine; no instrument verified solely by its own author
  • X1 — no naked work: every atom belongs to a workflow; nothing dispatches outside a workflow. A single atom is a workflow of one.
  • Intelligence floor: HOW undecided → the whole atom is handed to the LLM (RULED, A1-FINAL).
  • Verify templates: three pinned templates today — file-exists, grep-count, named-test (grounding, Work-Atom row).
  • Retry: loop canonical in s5 Lifecycle; budget A8 RULED as REC at the 2026-08-17 sitting (Quick eight: charter field retry_budget, default 3 — a labeled v1 dial).

Invariants

  • One goal, ONE verify — enforced at open (grounding: sutra-atom:267).
  • Atomic in accountability, not size: an internal step needing its own verify forces a split into two atoms in a workflow (RULED A1-ext).
  • X1 — no naked work: the atom always belongs to a workflow; a single atom is a workflow of one; nothing dispatches outside a workflow.
  • Intelligence floor: HOW undecided → the whole atom goes to the LLM (RULED A1-FINAL).
  • The attempt log is a data field on the HOW particle — never a ladder noun, never an object (RULED A1-FINAL-3).
  • C-state lives on the HOW particle (template side); promotions are earned by attempt evidence and are always gated, never auto (A9; sheet §2 GROW edge).
  • X2 — every attempt writes to some engine's record slice from run 1; the Engine (grown form of the Workflow) is born at the workflow's first attempt.

Ownership

  • Every atom belongs to exactly one workflow (X1).
  • Its attempt records belong to the owning engine's record slice (X2).
  • Today: ledger rows are keyed by session+atom, never by workflow — so no Engine is ever born from atom runs (grounding X2 row).

Output looks like

The persisted records an atom run produces: the atom.json object at open, and the run-ledger row appended at close/abandon. Field shapes follow the ratified v1 schema (sutra/os/native/schemas/run-ledger-row.schema.json, ADR-007 draft); the example below is the golden fixture row itself (byte-matched by test test_docs_match.sh), with lending-company values. The older sutra-atom ledger (.sutra/atom-ledger.jsonl, atom-ledger-v0.3) remains the runner’s own log and predates the ratified shape.
