Running a Department TWO LOOPS · 3 PICTURES

A department is a set of records until something moves it. This page is what moves it: the loop you walk to bring one into being, the loop it walks by itself afterwards, and the exact line between what fires while you sleep and what waits for you. Every box names a record or an actor that exists in the built app today; the one proposal on this page is marked as such and sits at the end.

1 · The owner's loop — one line to a running department

Four things come into being, and a stamp stands in front of each. Nothing in the row below happens without the stamp above it.

Edges: one line → stamp → the department and its folder → stamp → Identity → stamp → an engine with the moment it fires.

This is the recipe the Deckem department was born from on 2026-09-21, and the one the start-a-department workflow now replays: six writes, every one an ask the owner stamped. The story of that birth is on A Department's Life, section 8.

2 · The run loop — what happens after the stamp

This is the loop that repeats, and the only one that can run while you sleep.

Edges: the moment arrives → the engine runs in the folder → a work item → the check flips, so filed work → Audit checks the claim; the check does not flip → reopened and shown in Now; Audit → Adaptation asks for the next engine → your stamp → back to the moment arrives.

The circle closes at the stamp. A department gets better by asking, and the owner is the only one who can say yes — which is why the loop widens one engine at a time rather than drifting.

3 · What fires with nobody there

One row of this table is the whole of a department's autonomy today.

StepWithout a person?Why
An engine already enabledyesa calendar job outside the app runs it headless in its folder; a timer inside the app would never fire, because the app stops its own back end when the last window closes
A task already runningyesit keeps its own turns until it finishes, needs a decision, or hits its check; five at once, twenty at the ceiling
After the app restartsyeswhat was running is resumed and the queue drained at start-up
Creating or enabling an enginenoan ask, and an ask applies nothing until it is stamped
Any change to a recordnothe same: the ask is the only write path
A function's own chatnoa click always comes first; nothing in a department speaks on its own
A stamped engine is the only part of a department that acts while you are away. Autonomy is measured in engines, not in functions.

So a department runs itself exactly as far as its engines reach, and no further. Everything else is your loop. That is not a flaw in the build, it is the shape of the model as it stands: the five functions judge, order, hold and check, and the only thing with hands is an engine.

4 · The hole this leaves

Running a real department's own Identity chat three times, on the built screen, against the real records, showed the limit plainly: Identity judges, it does not make. Asked to build the thing its department exists for, it read, judged and reported. Nothing in the model hands that work to something with hands, so every piece of work passes through the owner. The run and its three gaps are written up in holding/plans/department-screen/RUN-A-DEPARTMENT.md.

5 · Proposed: the two kinds that would close the loop

Proposal, nothing built. The founder, 2026-09-23: a kind of function that creates — departments, engines, everything — and a kind that starts one. The name Maker was ruled by the founder the same day: it sits beside engine, work item and filed work without needing explanation, which is the test every word in this model has to pass.

The five that govern sit on one side and write only asks; the two that make sit on the other; the line between them is the stamp.

KindRuns whenJudged byMay writeEnds when
Starterthe department does not exist yetthe parent department's Identity and rulesnothing but asks, filed in the parentthe first engine is stamped
Makerthe department existsits own department's Identity and rulesfiles inside its own folder; records only by askthe work item's check flips

One rule separates them, and it is the rule the model already lives by: a function needs an Identity above it to judge what it does. A department being born has none of its own, so the parent's Identity judges its Starter. Everything made afterwards is judged by the department's own. If the founder prefers one kind to two, the Starter is simply the Maker's first job, run one level up.

Open gaps — founder queue

  • Two kinds or one. Starter as its own kind, or the Maker's first job run in the parent department.
  • What a Maker may do without asking. Write anything inside its own folder, with the folder as the fence, or file an ask per artifact and keep every step on the record.
  • Where a Maker's work is judged. Audit checks it after the fact, or Identity judges each effect before it lands, as it does today.
  • Departments minted without an ask. The app mints a department from every local project folder at start-up, outside the ask path; that is the one write into the tree that nobody stamps.
