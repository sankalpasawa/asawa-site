The life cycle of an engine and a department OBJECTS · zoom 4

The department screen shows a department at one instant. This page is the test that it holds across a life, from birth to retirement, for an engine and for a department. A stage holds when all five parts can be pointed at on the screen or in a rule. DRAFT

On the department screen, ruled 2026-09-20. One life cycle for both: eight stages for an engine, eleven for a department, the same five parts. A department is an engine that grew its own five functions. Screen: the department screen (journeys on it) (locked 2026-09-20; decision sheet section 12) · source record: session ideas/17.

s1. The five parts of a stage

PartMeaning
Triggerwhat starts the stage: a line, a row, a meter, a stamp
Statethe word the thing carries while in the stage: draft, live, paused, retired

s4. What holds, what does not

The design holds for birth, running, making, growing and splitting: E1, E2, E4, E7 and S1 to S4, S6, S7, S11. It does not hold at the two ends and in the middle of a life: an engine's own versions (E5), a paused engine (E6), retirement (E8, S9), a department whose functions never fire (S10), the threshold for "lived" (E3), and a parent's reading (S5). The screen follows the design exactly, so these are the design's gaps, not drawing errors.

s8. The five phases, and the four vocabularies reconciled

The eleven stages are events. A person speaks in phases, so the stages group into five, and each phase says what is true when it ends.

PhaseStagesTrue when the phase endsState word
1 IgniteS1somebody with authority has said this should exist, and the ask is stampeddraft
2 FormS2, S3it knows its goal, done line, rules, owner, budget and folder, and it has one enginelive
3 RunS4, S5work items open and close inside it; the done line can be read against filed worklive
4 HoldS6, S7, S8steady state: engines earn their place, overload splits off a child, the authority above may rewrite its ruleslive
5 EndS9, S10it has stopped, on purpose or by neglect, and its work has a successorretired

S11 is a case, not a stage: phases 1 and 2 collapsed into a company's own birth.

Four vocabularies describe this one object, and only one of them is enforced. That is why the phrase “life cycle” decides nothing today.

The phases and stages are for people; active and retired are for the record. A new state word must reach the record before a screen may show it, or it is decoration.

s9. Who may ignite one, and under whose authority

A department cannot judge its own birth, because it has no Identity yet. One rule covers every transition: the Identity above judges the thing changing. At birth that is the parent's Identity and rules; from S2 onward the department's own judges its effects, and only the authority above rewrites its rules.

The code is stricter than any page on four modules, and looser on one. A test proves that the four modules serving the tree, the reorganisation preview, the Org screen and the department screen never mint or retire a department, so a birth from any of them is always an engine act behind an ask, and the ask refuses unless the parent is live. The importer in the row above is the named exception: it lives outside that test and mints one department per project folder at start-up with nobody stamping; closing it is what the root department proposal is for. Two birth paths exist and no page yet names one as the path: the ask from the tree, and a department screen's own birth ask (an owner and two engines).

s10. The order the five functions come up

A function comes up when the record it reads first exists. Nothing else orders them. The order below is the usual one, not a law: a department born through a neighbour (the record proposes it, or another department's chat files it) meets Coordination before it has an engine, and a one-engine department nobody touches may never wake Coordination at all. A function wakes on events; it is not resident, and a card that stays empty is a fact about the department, not a fault.

OrderFunctionComes up whenBecause
1Identitythe birth ask is stampedit holds the goal, done line, rules, budget and owner, which every other function reads
2Adaptationthe goal existsit shapes the first engines; without it nothing can act
3Prioritythe first engine existsthere is nothing to order until something can run
4Coordinationtwo things can run at once, or a neighbour touches this oneholding and handing off is meaningless with one runner
5Auditthe first work is fileda check compares a claim against a record; before the first filed row there is no claim

Two consequences: a young department showing three empty cards is correct, not broken; and a department whose Audit never fires after filed work exists is in S10, which nothing detects today.
