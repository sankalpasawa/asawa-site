The structure: a system, three faces, six levels

The PRD has one shape. Everything in it is a system, the same shape at every level: what it is made of (parts), what it does over time (life), and how a person meets it (meetings). Six levels, zoomed. Every page and every idea has one cell: a level and a face. Neighbours are the other ideas in that cell.

Founder said (2026-10-01): "These four structures are not connecting with each other. They are not part of some structure which I chose." · "The focus right now is just to make the PRD out entirely, using the system's thinking to match it for all the ideas which I just gave you, and fitting that into a structure with its own abstraction levels. Work on that first, and then you can add placeholders for things which have not worked out for later." · (2026-09-30) "Adaptation is also like a system, right? It's also like a department of its own." · "I want to have a standardized template for each of these five functions."

Left: the one shape. Right: the six levels; a part of one level is the whole system at the next. The three faces exist inside every box.

The placing rule, and the card

Every idea, before any work, gets this card. It says the system before the instance, and whether the instance or the system is worked.

THIS IS:    the instance, in one line
ONE OF:     the kind, and the system that holds it   ->  level x face
WORK ON:    instance | the system too | the system first
BECAUSE:    the signal: it will repeat; the kind has no home; the fix would hide a missing rule
The kind is found, not picked (founder: "It doesn't need to be a life cycle, process, or journey. It can be something else. You need to figure that out."). When an idea fits no face at any level, that is said out loud and the axes are changed for that idea, then; never in advance, never a quiet extra bucket. Founder (2026-10-01): "Maybe we don't need to grow into that many axes, which are not required, but as and when we have a new idea, can we make sure that we modify the current axes?" The six families were asked about and set aside for this page: they stay the compass a department holds, not the faces of the structure. Where the card is said: by Claude when Sutra is made (the native builder's Understand phase, to make mandatory); by Adaptation when Sutra is used; by every born department when deployed. The last two are not solved yet, see the placeholders.

How we work: two ways of access, then a compromise

Founder (2026-10-01, verbatim): "I think we are designing from a user journeys and system journeys point of view, and then simultaneously we are componentizing into a system. That's how we are working. I think that's one way to go about system thinking. Whatever you want to do, you find an abstraction level in terms of practicalities of life. I think that is one other way of access, and then we try to compromise." And on the journeys named after the system's own moves: "Every rehash of the rules or engine from an idea: there are always crappy things, and they don't fit the actual. Give me a valid user journey."
