Reading the transport edge-contract

§5 is a transport layer, not a block of its own in the §1.0 schema: its lanes execute inside System of Process, and the things it talks to live in External World. That split is codex MODIFY-4's ownership rule — "Connector lanes live in SoP; External World owns counterparties" — and it is why this page declares edges instead of block internals. Traffic flows in two one-way lanes: outbound (B3 → B4) carries System of Process output through the Authority gate into a typed envelope with retry logic; inbound (B4 → B3) identity-verifies a counterparty's reply, wraps it into a typed envelope, and routes it back to B3 as the next action. People and services never meet inside the External World block — only inside System of Process. v1 reality per the inventory: Slack, Email (Resend), and Web are Live; Browser co-work is Charter TODO-005 (deferred v1.x); Phone is v2+; 3rd-party tools are pluggable via Composio with per-tool Charter scoping; Peer-Native A2A is a log-only stub until v2+. The B4 pillar (External comms) is the product face of this transport: "v1: actual transport via Connector layer" with DecisionProvenance + Artifact rows per message and Authority gating per stakeholder + tool + $-threshold (master §2.B B4).
