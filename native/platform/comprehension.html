Comprehension layer — Intent ⇆ Understanding PLATFORM · cross-cutting

STATUS: PLANNED · v2 · NOT BUILT. A cross-cutting platform capability (like Observability the ring, or Authority+Tenancy the wrap). It lives in the platform on purpose: every consumer product (CoS, Senior Expert, future) composes against its capability interface — re-homing + new build (deepen affordance, outbound mode, comprehension-memory), not free inheritance (see the review note below). Does not modify the LOCKED §1.0 schema; it annotates the Human↔System boundary.
Dual-lane peer review 2026-06-14 (codex + deepseek): CHANGES-REQUIRED, corrections noted. "Inherit" here means composition via a capability interface, not OO inheritance — it needs a concrete interface contract + versioning, and the pieces are partly NEW (deepen affordance, outbound mode, comprehension-memory), so this is re-homing + new build, not free inheritance. Open before build: comprehension-memory needs privacy / tenant-isolation / consent + a correction path; the explanation must be transactionally linked to the execution trace (else it is post-hoc rationalization that can diverge from what the system did); B1 intent currently lives in CoS, not the platform — the intent⇆understanding home is an open founder decision.

The semantic diagram — intent ⇆ understanding

INTENT flows down (you express → system acts); UNDERSTANDING flows up (system explains → you grasp). Same pipe, opposite directions — twins on the Human↔System hinge (the inbound twin is detailed at B1 Intent). Understanding is powered by HOW §3's lens climb, calibrated by the record, and rendered as progressive disclosure in the UI.

Why it lives in the platform — so every product inherits it

This is the whole reason it's a platform capability and not a CoS feature: build comprehension once in the platform behind an interface, and every product that adapts that interface gets it — though the interface + the new pieces (deepen-UI, outbound mode, comprehension-memory) must still be built once. Composition via an interface, not free inheritance.
