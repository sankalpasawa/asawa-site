Internal flow

The parts of the External World do not connect to each other — they are parallel counterparties, each reached over its own Connector channel. Traffic flows in two one-way lanes through the Connector transport (§5.2): on the outbound lane, System of Process output (B3's decision, B4's draft) passes the Authority gate, becomes a typed envelope with retry logic, and lands on a service endpoint (Slack, email, web, 3rd-party tool) or, through Slack/email, on a human stakeholder; on the inbound lane, a human's reply (e.g. the board chair's Slack response) is identity-verified, wrapped into a typed envelope, and routed back to B3 as the next action — so people and services only "meet" inside System of Process, never inside the External World block. Peer Sutras have only a log-only A2A stub edge in v1 (no real transport), and browser/phone counterparts have no live edge at all. At the §1.0 altitude the whole block collapses to a single bidirectional "context + effects" edge with System of Process, with Authority + Tenancy wrapping every crossing.
