The product, in the order it is built MOCKUPS · PROPOSED

To be re-derived. On 2026-09-26 the founder moved the world to later and redefined five capabilities; these screens still follow the earlier order. The design they will be re-derived from is from capabilities to a system that runs.

What a person sees as the system is built in the order ruled on what comes first: the same steps as the system, grown in this order, shown as the product's screens. Whatever a screen adds is outlined in yellow and tagged with its step; what is ruled next is drawn faint and tagged NEXT.

Founder's direction, 2026-09-26: keep what comes first; keep the system, grown in this order; derive the product and the growth to a company from it, and merge the rest. This page replaces the product, one family at a time (archived), whose screens added one family per step rather than following the ruled order.
