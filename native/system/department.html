One department, grown as a product A READING · PROPOSED

The blueprint of one department, Collections, in the structure Core already has: one system whose five functions are internal systems, each running on its own, with the charter held inside Identity, the budgets inside Priority and the timetable inside Coordination; engines between artifacts; System status and runs. Every part is drawn faint from the start; each step fills parts in, in the ruled order, until the whole department works.

Founder's direction, 2026-09-27: keep the implemented structure; grow one department at a time, starting small; show the runtime behaviour behind each change, the workflows and the artifact pieces; the charter lives in Identity; context comes from artifacts and actions plus the company's, and is open. Then: the same shape as the system, grown in this order, drawn as a blueprint of the product. Later the same day: a department starts when its parent publishes it and its Identity is running; the charter is Identity's own state, not a separate card; the flow runs in sequence with nobody there, and a person comes in only to approve, to ask Identity for something extra, or through the parent's cut-offs; workflows, artifact types and rules come from a parallel making line, found by walking up from the department to the root. Then: the parent's Adaptation, not its Identity, spins out a new department; routines are engines; the human part is a minimal set of controls. Then: Coordination keeps the timetable among its other jobs; an engine starts its own runs; Priority grants budgets; everything Beer recommends is put in.

Each step names its lifecycle stage (the life cycle, which this page links to and does not redefine), its status (built in Core, partly built, designed), and the signal, workflow and ask behind it.
