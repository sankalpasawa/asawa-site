The first build: a website department, and the Map BUILT FOR ONE DEPARTMENT · WALKED 2026-09-27

The design on one department, grown is the baseline. This page turns it into the first thing to build: one department that builds a website on its own, and the screens that let anyone see everything inside it. It is built in Sutra Desktop for this one department, and was walked as a person would walk it, with the real model writing the site. Section 0 says what is built, what is not, and where the proof is.

Founder's direction, 2026-09-27: log the high-level design; check that we reached step 8; wherever there is a long list, make structures of it; pause growth; take the minimum an autonomous system needs to build a website; design how users see all of it in the app (a lay of the land under the organization, fitted into today's UI); and how anyone knows it works, with nothing hidden. Then: build nothing; write the PRD and the UI designs, and update the page. Then: combine the priorities of the release-state pages into one list; check the desktop app and design every screen as a change to it; and make anything we make viewable in the app on the fly. Then: make it work end to end in the Mac app with the smallest change to it; one department only; in a new organisation with its root department; with the Library's templates; with a view for the motor and the internal systems; stop when a person can give a goal and the department runs on its own.

0 · What is built

One command in the Org screen's Edit menu founds an organisation, its root department and a Website department. Given a goal, the department plans, writes and checks a site on its own, asks once before the first publish, and after that grows the site on every ask with nobody stamping. It is released in Sutra Desktop 2.306.0 (Mac and Windows, 2026-09-28) and was walked in the installed app that day, in a new organisation in the owner's own registry.

What the build changed in the app

Two new server modules, one new screen script, two suites (9 and 12 named tests), and nine hook lines in four existing files. Every hook answers nothing unless the selected department has a website record, so every other department paints exactly as before. The files and the design are in holding/plans/website-department/: PRD, DESIGN and WALK.

What the walk found that no test had: a server told to stop kept ticking beside the new one, and one slot ran twice. The Health check named it (Slots, red) on a real record. The motor now holds an operating-system lock, so one record has one motor, and a closing app stops ticking at once.

4 · PRD: a department that builds a website

Problem

Today nothing makes one department run end to end on its own. Runs are fired by a job outside the app, a version does not say what it was made from, and a person cannot see what is where inside a department or tell whether it is working.

Who

The owner, who creates a department from one line and wants it to run itself; and anyone let in, who wants to see what is happening without asking.

The minimum: the Website department

Four engines in a line, five artifacts, three of the five systems on, the motor, and the human surface. The brief is the only input a person gives.

7 · How you know it works, with nothing hidden

A department is working when seven checks pass, each computed from records and drawn as a dot in the map's health row. A red dot opens the view that explains it.

CheckPasses whenOpens
Motor alivethe last tick is under a minute oldthe motor bar
Slots keptevery slot in the period ran once: none missed twice, none ran twicethe timeline
Versions wholeevery version has its run, its inputs and a check verdictthe trace
Nothing stuckno ask or wait older than its limit, nothing escalatedSystem status
Systems awake righteach system is awake exactly when its record existsthe five dots
Inside budgetevery engine inside its envelopePriority's box
Moving to donethe done reading moved toward the goal since the last periodthe done bar

The trace lives on every filed thing (screen 9) and every run (screen 8); the timeline, the motor and the checks live in Health (screen 12).

Six things may never happen, and each one alarms in System status the moment it does: a run with no slot, a version with no run, a slot that ran twice, a retry not counted on its run, an AI exchange missing from the run's chat, a skip with no reason row.

Nothing hidden: every run is shown while it runs and kept as a row after; every AI exchange is in its run's chat (D81); every change to the department is a version with a decision row; every skip and retry has a row with its reason.

8 · What this makes harder

The map shows everything, so a department with many engines will crowd it; the first build has four, and the crowding question waits for growth. The health checks need limits (how old an ask may get) that nobody has set; the PRD leaves them to the charter. And two pages now describe the department: this one is the build, the department page is the design; a change to the design must be read here too.

s1 · The customer

Native’s customer is one person who runs an entire company. Not a team that coordinates — a single operator whose departments run as systems that ask for what they need, earn autonomy rung by rung, and improve themselves — while the person decides. Running example: one founder operating a digital lending company — origination, KYC, disbursal, collections, marketing — alone, because every department is a Native system.

Every feature below is phrased as what this person can now do. The objective the product is measured against: the company runs; the person decides.
