Testing as a person: the Human Simulation program DRAFT

A goal and a person. An agent drives the installed Sutra Beta by clicks alone, as that person would, checks each step against the person's expectation, writes every miss as a row with a capture, and walks again after the fix ships. The founder's words, 2026-09-28: "get hooked, click one step, and see if it matches the expectation ... note down, then fix, and then again test, end to end."

Solid green: the step matched, walk on. Red dashed: a miss becomes a row, a fix, a release, then the walk runs again from the same step.
