The Frame ONE PICTURE · A READING

Six families say what gets set. Four stages say how it becomes real. Three actors say who does it. Four moves carry it between levels and along time; a base case above the root and the atom below are where they stop. One picture, one notation, and every named axis with where it stands in the Mac app today.

A reading, not a ruling (2026-09-25). It assembles what the axes, life-cycle, builders and work-atom pages already rule. Decide, Delegate, Deliver and Propose are the plain words proposed for ignition, propagation and execution, and are the founder's to confirm. The base case, the back move and the term cell were proposed or found on 2026-09-25 after the in-house review of the last 23 iterations (section 4); they are recorded as the life-cycle PRD's calls 9, 10 and 11, and the ruled box on the axes page is untouched.

1 · The whole frame, in one picture

Read it top to bottom for Realization and Delegate, bottom to top for Reflection and Propose. The coloured strip in every box is the six families: every level carries all six, and every level has a goal and a check. Root is a proposal (the root department); the four levels below it are built.
