Step 0

The goal, and the rule of the cut

Everything starts from one line with its own check. The cut has one rule: keep cutting until each piece has one goal and one check and cannot be split and still be work. That smallest piece is a work atom.

THE GOALGive one customer a small personal loan.
Check: the money is in the customer's account within 2 working days, and every step is on record.
THE RULE OF THE CUT1. Choose an axis: the question you cut on.
2. Cut the goal into pieces along it.
3. Each piece gets one goal and one check.
4. Stop when a piece cannot be split and still be work.
5. Add the next axis; the pieces become a grid.
THE SIX QUESTIONS (the compass) SHAPE what pieces, cut fresh from this ask?
TIME when, and on what beat?
EDGE who hands what to whom?
GOVERN may this step go: auto, ask or deny?
RESOURCE what finite stock does it spend?
GROW what has the record taught, across runs?

We start with the two easiest to see, PART and TIME, because a person can draw them on paper. The other four are added one at a time on the same grid.

The rule of the cut is the Realization move of the compass: down, until the atom.page 2 of 10

One cell, read on all six axes

Take one subgoal, "read the bank statements and pull the bureau score". It is not six things; it is one thing with six readings. This is what the system reads on every cell, every run.

Do it yourself

How to cut any goal, in five moves

  1. Write the goal in one line, with its check. If you cannot say how you would know it is done, it is a wish, not a goal.
  2. Cut on PART. Follow the natural seams. Give each piece one goal and one check. Stop when a piece cannot split and still be work.
  3. Cut on TIME. Put each piece in its phase. Leave empty cells empty.
  4. Add one reading at a time: OWNER (who, and the hand-offs), AUTHORITY (auto, ask, deny), RESOURCE (what it spends, by when).
  5. Run it, read every cell, and let GROW change the next cut. A step that never varies freezes. A stamp given the same way ten times becomes a rule. A pain that repeats grows a new management rung.
WHY TWO AXES FIRSTPART and TIME are the two anyone can draw. They make the goal runnable on their own. The other four do not add pieces; they add readings to the pieces that exist.
WHY THE FAMILIES ARE SIX AND CLOSEDEvery question you can ask of work sorts into one of them: reads the input fresh (SHAPE), accumulates on the unit (GROW), gates a step (GOVERN), reads a link (EDGE), spends a stock (RESOURCE), is about when (TIME). New axes are minted freely inside a family; the six do not change.
THE PROMISEFirst run expensive, every run cheaper. Not a hope: the grid records every cell, and GROW reads the record.
