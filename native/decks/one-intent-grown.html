Try it on someone

A case question that leads a person to this picture

Give this to someone who has never seen the compass. Ask the follow-ups in order, only when they stall. A strong answer grows the same picture on its own.

THE QUESTIONA friend has just started a small lending company. One product: personal loans of 1 to 5 lakh for salaried people the banks turn away. She has three staff, a laptop, and no process. She wants a company that can give out a loan without her touching it.

Organise her work so that: (a) a loan is paid out within two working days; (b) she only looks at the things that truly need her; (c) the hundredth loan costs less to process than the first; and (d) after a hundred loans she knows better than today whom to lend to.

Draw it. You may ask me anything about her business.

Scoring: a check on the goal; pieces with their own checks; a grid with honest empty cells; named hand-offs; auto versus ask; a cost per piece; a learning loop that changes the cut.
