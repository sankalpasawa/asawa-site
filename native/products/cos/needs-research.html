§10.3 Holding Company Operating Model

Rationale: Sutra is not a product FOR companies — Asawa is a company that CONTAINS other companies. Holding-company research concluded Asawa is closer to LVMH/Tata than Berkshire/Alphabet: centralized shared infrastructure + decentralized brand autonomy + portfolio-level capital allocation. Exactly 3 functions must be owned at holding level: Governance Office · Portfolio Office · Shared Infrastructure. Everything else is Unit-scoped.
