§A.1 Problem statement (founder-delegated working voice, filled 2026-07-27)

I run many streams of work across multiple companies, but a founder's throughput scales sub-linearly with the team — every hire adds coordination cost, not just capacity. The assistants that exist are either conversational (answer, then forget) or chore-based (one narrow task on command). Nothing compounds: no tool accumulates my context, my judgment, and my way of working into leverage that grows. I need a system where the work itself compounds across everything I do. [founder-delegated working voice — Claude-synthesized 2026-07-27; correct freely]

This paragraph is the WHY apex: the 41 needs below decompose it. The press-release framing of the same WHY lives on the hub (§0 — evidence only, not duplicated here; primary home = hub per codex MODIFY-2). Source: master §A.1.
