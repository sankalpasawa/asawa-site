§5 Connector — transport layer #

PurposeMove messages + tool calls between Native (§3) and the world outside (humans, services, peer Sutras).
OwnsSlack · email · web · browser · phone · 3rd-party tool integrations · inbound/outbound message handling · retry semantics.
Depends on§3 Authority gate (at every send); §6 Observability (for delivery health).
Does NOT ownWhat to say (B3); decision logic (B3); Charter scope (§3); audience selection (B4 + Authority).

   Every receive: Identity-verified · typed-envelope · routed to B3 next action.

§5.3 Acceptance criteria (Connector v1)

  • Every outbound call passes through Authority gate; default-deny on missing scope.
  • Inbound from unverified identity logs + drops, doesn't enter B3.
  • Transient failures retry; permanent failures surface to operator (no silent drop).
  • Per-tool scoping enforced via Charter (e.g. "Slack:#paisa-team only" not "Slack:*").
