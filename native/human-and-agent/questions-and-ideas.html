Questions and ideas HUMAN AND AGENT · the appendix

Every question and idea the founder raised on this subject, in one place: written up plainly, his own words under each, and a pointer to the page and section that answers it. The pages keep their flow; this is the index of what they owe.

Status: answered the pages hold the answer; partly the frame is there, the measurement is not; open named, not yet worked. The days are 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08.

B. What agent orgs are, and what they can make

#The question or idea, written upWhere it is answeredStatus
B1What are the productized layers of a harness that runs workflows, their combinations, coordination and priority, and what level of problem does each solve? Skill-level problems at the atom and workflow; management-level at engine, function and department; the economic problem at the agent org and the market.
in his words
What will be the productized use cases of this kind of harness which allows for workflows, a combination of workflows, and coordination priority, and to what level they can solve? I think they are trying to solve management-level problems and execution.
The future organisation, six levels; Futures, the industryanswered
B2What outcomes can a group of agents on this harness create? A closed list from the runs: artifacts, decisions, rules, engines, records, and at rung 4 trades; a seventh, promises with a date, is open.
in his words
There is a group of agents running on this kind of harness. What are the possible outcomes they can create?
The experiment, output kindspartly
B3An agent org (AO), a meta-AO of AOs, one with every human, AOs interacting; what money an AO needs. An AO spends four stocks and sells one outcome; the meta-AO is Native's EDGE and RESOURCE families lifted a level plus disputes; whether an AO holds money itself is open.
in his words
Let's call that an agent org. Let's call it AO. There are various AOs and the combination of AOs, which is the meta AO. They are there with every human, and AOs have to interact with each other as well. What kind of financial requirements are there for AOs?
The future organisation, AO money and the meta-AOpartly
B4A civilization of humans and AIs: needs set the direction, AIs solve the wants, and AIs have wants that humans and other AIs solve. Drawn as the wants loop; one hop seen in the runs, the second hop is the experiment.
in his words
An AI civilization where there are both humans and AIs. How do they interact with each other to drive the human needs as a fundamental directional level and the human wants? AIs are trying to solve those human wants, and to solve for those human wants, the AIs also have wants which other AIs, plus humans, are solving.
The future organisation, the wants looppartly
B5Exactly what the AI does and what the human does, as an instance under the labels, in Sutra's own words. One real run traced through the five functions, the record and the stamps, with a who column.

Give how exactly a particular want of a human is satisfied by the AO and by the human. It's not very clear what exactly AI is and what is human. There are some words, but those words are also at a label level.
The experiment, one want, one instanceanswered
B6What kinds of AI get created along the whole lifecycle, from small to large, and where not; where to cut the problem so the types can be defined. Cut by size and by stage; industry is the instance; a thirty-cell grid with the built, the next and the human cells.
in his words
What kind of AI types will be created around the entire lifecycle of AI, from small to large, and also in which it is not? How do we fragment the problem statement so that we can define and create the lifecycle of various types? On what line should we cut that out?

B7Pockets of AO markets, the SaaS of AO: AOs that make and serve AOs, deployed to all of them, combining pairwise as the market needs. A pocket is one cell of the grid sold across agent orgs; the pair count grows as the square; the market prices the pairs.
in his words
We will have markets for those AOs for those particular units, and those will be the various small pockets of AO markets. That will be like the SaaS of AO, like the AOs which are working towards creating various AOs. There will be NC2 kinds of combinations depending on the market needs.

C. The future, the economy, the markets

#The question or idea, written upWhere it is answeredStatus
C1How the industry and the economy shape up at H1; how each industry solves human needs; how much AOs take and how human orgs remain; what wants come next. Four layers; thinking cheap and used more, body and trust dear; nine needs keep their industries and lose their middle; three sorting questions; the next wants are about trust.
in his words
How the industry will shape up in 5 years' time. How the economy will shape up. Understanding various industries which solve human needs: how will they solve them in the new world? How agents/AOs will take a significant share of solving human needs and wants, and how human orgs will remain. If those wants are solved by AOs, then what are the future wants which will arise?
Futures at H1answered
C2A visual map of one market at H2: one industry, one aspect, the hardware, the other industries, the logistics, how it fits, with numbers. A skin condition treated, one morning: eleven parties, two of them people, nine flows; six other industries on the same star; sourced numbers to 2030, projected to H2.
in his words
I wanted visual maps of the markets in the final year. One industry, one aspect of it, various other components of the world view that are there, hardware, other kinds of industries, logistics, how it fits there. Then some quantitative numbers around us, so I can understand how the market will be structured in the future to serve human needs.
Market map at H2answered
C3How much of the economy do the known references say gets automated; which markets open in India given the global and Indian market structure. Eight references on two scales; India's structure (informal, services, MSMEs, software exports, UPI); five markets ranked by measurability; assumptions A1 to A9.
in his words
I also want to take inspiration from other people out there on how much of the economy they see getting automated by AI. In the end, I want to model it out: what kind of markets will open up in India, given the structure of the market, global and in India.
Automation and Indiapartly
C4The market structure and human needs stay the same; markets speed up; industries with many humans and high cost get rejigged; second- and third-order effects. Held as assumptions T1 to T4; the effects in a table with where each is handled.
in his words
Definitely, the market structure is not going to change. Human needs are not going to change significantly. Maybe a lot of markets would get speeded up based on the existing technology. Entire industries which have a lot of humans, a lot of cost, will get rejigged. There are a lot of second-order and third-order effects.
Lifecycle and transition, section 3partly
C5The transition: diffusion is uneven; which industries get the jerk and the chaos; which leap straight to the new ecosystem because software never worked there. Four diffusion shapes from two causes: leap, slide, jerk, stall; H1 at the jerk's peak.
in his words
The diffusion is not going to be the same, and because of the diffusion, which industries are going to get a lot of overkill, a lot of jerk? There is a lot of chaos in the existing companies trying to change to the new ways of doing things. There will be certain industries which will take a significant jump directly towards the new ecosystem because there are initial barriers where the technology did not provide that much subjectivity, that much determinism.
Lifecycle and transition, section 3answered
