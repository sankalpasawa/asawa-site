Proof and the experiment HUMAN AND AGENT

What is proven today, one real run traced step by step; the five rungs that test the rest; the human seat measured; and the calls that are the founder's.

Proof today: one want, one instance, in Sutra's own words

The labels above (AO, the seat, a stamp, a function) are abstractions. This section is one instance of them, taken from a real run on the Sutra Beta: run 3 of the Human Simulation, 2026-09-29, 15:10 to 15:54. The founder's want, in his words: "Best dermatologists in Bangalore as an example of a department." Forty-four minutes later a site was live, four versions in, with a source line under every fact. The run is on record in qa/sim/runs/dermatologists-bangalore-20260929-1509 (24 ledger rows, findings 34 to 46).

First, the department as departments of departments: where memory and understanding live

A department on Sutra is not one agent. It is a small organisation of five functions (the five functions), each a role that owns engines, plus a record. The founder's verbs from over the months map onto them like this:

The run as lanes: who did what, in order

Why this picture: it is the record, not a design; and why the person's lane is so thin
  • Claim. A real want was satisfied by a department on Sutra with the person doing eight small acts, and every other act is attributable to a named function or engine.
  • Evidence. The 24 ledger rows and captures of runs/dermatologists-bangalore-20260929-1509; the findings 34 to 46 filed from it; the fixes shipped in v2.306.17. Nothing in the lanes is invented; the two misses the run also showed (a site built before the engine existed, 34; the engine searching four times for one ask, 38) are in the table below.
  • Why the lanes are functions, not agents. The founder's "departments of departments": a department is five roles that own engines, with a record between them. Drawing it as one agent would hide where memory (the Record) and understanding (Comprehension, Identity) live.
  • Why the person's lane is thin. The three sorting questions (3.4): the person acts only where no machine can check (the want, the rule, the taste in "top three first") or where someone must be liable (the stamps on what goes out).
  • What would change it. If the next run needs a human to do an engine's work by hand (finding 56's "by hand" step), the lane is not thin and the label "AO solves the want" is not earned for that department.

Why this table: what the labels mean once they have an instance, and where the run fell short
  • "AO solves the want" means steps 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 12: reading, deciding an engine is missing, shaping it, searching, building, checking. Six of twelve steps, and all the minutes.
  • "The human seat" means steps 1, 3, 6, 8, 11: the want, three stamps, one direction, one rule. Five acts, each under a minute. Step 10 is the mixed case: the person's taste, the AO's work.
  • "Memory" means the Record's rows in this run: Brief v1 to v4, the Source Reader v1 filing, the rule, the engine in the Library. Nothing the person had to remember.
  • "Understanding" means step 2 and step 4: the words read into a department with a goal, and the goal read for what it needs. That is Comprehension plus Identity, not a model's general knowledge.
  • Where it fell short. The line tried to publish before the engine had filed (34); the engine searched four times when one was asked (38, about 2 USD each); Audit asked the person for facts (41); a re-search shrank the sources (46). All four became rows N-024 to N-027 and were fixed in 2.306.17. The labels are earned only because the misses are on the record too.
  • What would change it. A run where a step marked AO needs a person to finish it by hand. Then that step moves to the person's lane and the share in 3.4 for this industry is lower than drawn.

Why five rungs, and why rung 3 is next
  • Why rungs. Each adds exactly one new thing to a run that already works: a second AO, then a price, then many. One new thing per rung is what makes a miss attributable.
  • Why rung 3 first. It is the cheapest test of the page's central claim (AOs will need each other) and needs no new party: two website departments, two persons, one wait gate lifted from N-024.
  • Why kill lines. A rung without a kill line is a demo. Each line names the result that would refute the claim, so the founder can read the outcome without the author in the room.
  • Evidence. The program's shape (person + goal, every miss a row, fix, release, rerun) is the one that produced findings 1 to 64 across runs 1 to 5.

Output kinds an AO can create, a closed list from the runs so far: artifacts (a site, a report, a page); decisions (an ask answered); rules (stamped, filed); engines (born from words); records (filings, versions); and in rung 4, trades (a price stated and accepted). Open: a seventh kind, a promise with a date, held by the meta-AO.

The human seat

From the runs, the human's acts are few and of one shape: a stamp on a line, a sentence in a chat, a rule, a decline. This is the April 2026 vision's autonomy slider (long-term direction) read per act instead of per function. Section 9 shows every one of these acts in one run.

Why the seat is taste, money and liability, and why 5 to 10 minutes
  • Why these acts. They are every act a person took in runs 1 to 5, listed from the ledgers. Nothing is added from theory.
  • Why the time. One stamp per line, zero to two answers, one rule when it mattered: at the runs' pace that is minutes, not hours, per department per day.
  • Why liability is the floor. It is the only row an AO cannot take under any of the three sorting questions, so it is what the seat is for once everything else moves.
  • What would change it. Rung 5: if owners of 5 to 10 departments give over 30 minutes a day and still block the line, the seat is a bottleneck and the product must batch it.

Inference The seat is taste, money and liability, about 5 to 10 minutes per department per day at rungs 2 and 3. Rung 5 reads whether that holds at 5 to 10 departments; the kill line is 30 minutes a day.
