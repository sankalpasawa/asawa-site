Edges — the typed links between atoms OVERVIEW · sub-page

WORKING DRAFT (2026-06-15). Work-atoms are the nodes; an edge is the link between two of them. A workflow is nodes + edges. Edges come in three kinds — each born from a different shaping choice and each needing different handling.

The three kinds

EdgeMeaningBorn fromNeedsExample
handoffA's output → B's inputsequence (A before B)order + a usable outputaudit → draft
shared-resourceA & B want one finite thingconcurrency on a finite poolan allocation rule (FCFS/priority/budget)two atoms, one rate-limit → one waits
must-cohereA & B each make a piece of one wholeone output split into parallel partsa standard up-front, or an integration step3 screens, one visual style
Each edge is the flip side of a shaping choice: sequence makes handoffs, concurrency on a finite thing makes shared-resource edges, decomposition of one output makes must-cohere edges. Shared-resource edges are the Resource axis seen between two atoms — the S2/S3 maturation rungs.

Where edges live

Edges are wired at SHAPE (the workflow shape is nodes + edges) and grown at GROW (the cross-unit axis decides who-may-call-whom). Isolation is the hard default; an edge is the only sanctioned exception — a cross-unit edge is an explicit, revocable grant, not ambient access.

  • Worked example with all three in one run: Company decomposed.
  • The contention side (allocation): Resource · Time (scheduling contention).
  • Overview + the loop: the system, simply.
