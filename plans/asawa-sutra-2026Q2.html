01

Company Plan

Draft

Asawa Inc.

Wedge
One-operator holding company that runs its portfolio through an in-house AI system (Sutra) instead of hiring a team per product.
12-month bet
Prove that one founder + AI can profitably operate a 6-product portfolio (DayFlow, Billu, Paisa, PPR, Maze, Reflection) without full-time staff per company.
Customer
Myself, as the operator. Through Sutra, the broader fleet of solo builders.
Moat
Depth of governance baked into Sutra + the daily loop of using it on real businesses, not toy demos.
Kill criteria
If, at 12 months, combined inflow from products + Sutra is below my personal burn + infra, I change shape.

Sutra

Wedge
An operating system that weighs context and skills for AI work. Core IP is the weighting engine — not "another agent framework."
12-month bet
500+ active installs and 10+ paying customers; Asawa products as daily proving ground.
Customer
AI-leveraged builders and operators in the early-adopter market.
Moat
The weighting IP plus visible discipline of using it on real businesses.
Kill criteria
If, at 12 months, active installs are below 500 OR no one has paid for it, the shape changes.

04

Process — the operating system

Live
What's already running
Sutra (the operating system) ships me + the fleet a consistent set of governance, skills, and review tools. Internal departments (analytics, etc.) track what's working. Daily and per-company sessions follow the same structure.
Continuous principle
If I have to remember to do something, it's not done yet — turn it into a hook or skill so it happens automatically.
Review discipline
Every important plan or change passes through at least one independent AI critic (Codex and/or DeepSeek) before it's locked.
Why orthogonal
Process runs alongside everything else; it doesn't gate the other blocks but quietly compounds their quality over time.
Depends on nothing (orthogonal) Feeds all other blocks (quality multiplier)
