The question everyone asks

"Why won't Anthropic just build this?"

Three reasons
They say so themselves. The Claude Agent SDK exists so developers can build agents on top of Claude Code. And they gave away the plumbing — MCP, open-sourced, then donated to the Linux Foundation alongside OpenAI.
They contractually can't hold it. "By default, we will not use your inputs or outputs to train our models." The asset this layer runs on is your decision history — and they promised 300,000 customers they wouldn't accumulate it.
When a lab tries, it retreats. OpenAI shipped Agent Builder to own the enterprise agent layer in October 2025. It shuts down this November.

HONEST  They are already partway here. The risk isn't that they build something better — it's that they build something good enough and bundle it free.

What it takes to build one

Autonomy is not installed.
It is built, one loop at a time.

GOVERN — EVERY STEP IS AUTO, ASK, OR DENY

SEE IT

Every decision lands in the record automatically — so you automate the real process, not the imagined one.

⚑ you can query what happened

SHAPE IT

Frame first, then break the work down — each piece carrying the check that will prove it, written before work starts.

⚑ every step has a done-test

RUN IT

Work executes and verifies itself. Nothing counts as finished until its own check passes; gates pause only for your calls.

⚑ audit trail and a tested rollback

KEEP IT

The shape becomes a charter with an owner, a trigger and a switch-off rule — and the record makes the next loop better.

⚑ owner, trigger, kill switch

Skip the first and you automate a process nobody runs. Skip the second and agents report "done" with no proof. Skip the fourth and you are the scheduler, forever.

Same workflow, two ways to run it — this is the cost of the function, not the cost of one employee.

This is not a cheaper-than-a-person argument. One Mumbai analyst costs a fraction of that pod, and less than we do. We are cheaper than the function, and cheaper than the heads you would add next year.

And the pod does not stay. Frontline attrition in Indian financial services runs from roughly half the team to the whole team, every year. The process knowledge leaves with them. Software does not resign.
